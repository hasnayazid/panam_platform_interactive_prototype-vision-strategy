How Keycloak Powers the Ecosystem
  • One Login Across Cycles: Athletes and officials use one account across multiple Pan Am editions instead of creating new logins every time.
  • Role-Based Security (RBAC): Keycloak strictly separates what a PANAM Admin, an NOC Manager, a Sponsor, and an Athlete can see.
  • Future Multi-Federation Ready: In Phase 2 (Post-Sale), Bornan can spin up separate Keycloak realms for OCA and FISU in minutes.
